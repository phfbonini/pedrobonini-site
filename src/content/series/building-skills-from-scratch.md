This series walks through what I've learned designing Skills for engineering teams at Elotech. From the conceptual basics — what a Skill actually is, and why the abstraction matters — to operational concerns like distribution, versioning, and knowing when a Skill is doing more harm than good.

The posts are roughly chronological and build on each other, but each one stands alone if you have a specific question.
